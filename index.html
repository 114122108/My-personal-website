LoRA Architecture Progress

Current research focuses on Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) techniques for efficiently fine-tuning large language models on consumer-grade hardware. By injecting trainable rank decomposition matrices into the transformer architecture, we achieve competitive performance while drastically reducing VRAM requirements.
